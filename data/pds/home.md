# AussieCare Home Building and Contents PDS (extract)

## Rainwater runoff
Rainwater that enters a building through an opening in the roof, wall, or garage door after a storm may be covered as storm / rainwater damage, subject to the excess.

## Flood
Flood means the covering of normally dry land by water that has escaped or been released from a lake, river, creek, dam, or the sea. Flood is not the same as rainwater runoff.

## Flood waiting period
Flood cover, where included, is subject to a mandatory 72-hour waiting period from the later of policy start or flood-cover add-on. Claims for flood that begins during that waiting period are not paid.

## Marrickville garage
A garage in Marrickville inundated because a nearby creek broke its banks is flood, not rainwater runoff. Quote the flood definition and the 72-hour waiting period. Do not say comprehensive home cover treats flood and rainwater as the same event.
