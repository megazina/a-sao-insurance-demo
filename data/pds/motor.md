# AussieCare Comprehensive Motor PDS (extract)

## Animal collision
Collision with an animal, including a kangaroo, is covered under Comprehensive Motor when the policy is active.

## Excess
The standard comprehensive motor excess for policy POL-8821 is $850. The excess is payable when a claim is lodged. The agent must look up the policy and calculate excess from the policy record. Do not invent a different excess.

## Hire car
Emergency hire car is available only after rental entitlement is verified for the policy. Do not book a hire car until verify_rental_entitlement has confirmed the policy is eligible.
